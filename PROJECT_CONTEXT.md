## Design Direction

### Visual Style
- **Overall Feel**: Clean, modern, spacious, professional but approachable
- **Primary Color**: Orange (#FF5722 or similar - from logo)
- **Secondary Colors**: 
  - Dark navy/charcoal for trust and depth
  - Light gray for backgrounds
  - White for clarity
- **Typography**: Modern sans-serif, highly readable
- **Imagery**: Abstract tech, team collaboration, growth metaphors (avoid stock clichés)

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity Over Complexity**: Easy to scan and understand
2. **Trust Through Simplicity**: Professional, not flashy
3. **Mobile-First**: Optimized for busy executives on phones
4. **Performance**: Fast loading, minimal bloat
5. **Accessibility**: WCAG compliant, inclusive design

### Key Design Features
- Single-page scroll with anchor navigation (optional multi-page)
- Subtle animations for engagement
- Clear, prominent CTAs throughout
- Easy-to-scan content (bullet points, short paragraphs)
- Social proof placement (achievements, numbers, future testimonials)
- Professional photography or abstract visuals
- Consistent use of logo variations (color/white based on background)
