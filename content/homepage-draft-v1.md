## SECTION 2: THE CHALLENGE

### Heading
**Growing Pains Are Real. You Don't Have to Navigate Them Alone.**

### Introduction
Scaling a tech company isn't just about adding more engineers. It's about building the right foundation—technical, cultural, and organizational—that enables sustainable growth.

### Pain Points (3 cards/columns)

**Challenge 1: Team Performance, Alignment & Scale**  
Your engineering team isn't delivering consistently. There's misalignment between what engineering builds and what the business needs. As the team grows, coordination becomes harder and quality slips. You need changes that actually stick—not just another reorganization that fades in weeks.

**Challenge 2: Platform Scaling & Architecture**  
Your platform worked great at first, but now you're hitting limits. Performance degrades under load. Enterprise clients need capabilities you can't deliver. You need to scale your architecture without over-engineering—and without accumulating technical debt that will slow you down later.

**Challenge 3: Modern Tech Without the Hype**  
You're hearing about AI, microservices, event-driven architecture—but what actually makes sense for your business? You need to evolve your tech stack strategically, not chase every trend. The key is knowing when to adopt new technology and when to optimize what you have.
